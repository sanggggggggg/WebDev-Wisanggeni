5 FACTS ABOUT THE UNIVERSE

The Universe is Expanding

First theorised by Georges Lemaître, Edwin Hubble confirmed the expansion of the universe by observing the redshift of galaxies.

The Universe Has Three Possible Shapes

The Universe has three theorised shapes depending on the density of matter and energy: closed (spherical), flat (Euclidean), and open (hyperbolic).

The Universe Expands Faster Than Light

Since the expansion rate of the Universe at a point is proportional to the distance from an observer, extremely distant galaxies may expand faster than the speed of light!

Ordinary Matter is Extremely Rare

Only approximately 5% of the Universe is ordinary (baryonic) matter, the rest being dark matter and dark energy.

Galaxies are Not Spread Out Randomly

If you zoom out, the Universe is actually made up superclusters of galaxies, and large voids. This pattern creates a web-like shape.
